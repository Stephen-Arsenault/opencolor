About OpenColor

OpenColor is a free browser app that reads a Datacolor ColorReader (CR100) colorimeter over Web Bluetooth and matches the measured CIELAB color to named standards — RAL Classic, Pantone (PMS Coated & Uncoated), NCS, Munsell, and paint fandecks from Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore — ranked by CIEDE2000. No vendor app or account required.

Works with

The Datacolor ColorReader is sold under several paint-brand labels built on the same CR100 hardware — PPG, Benjamin Moore, RAL, Dunn-Edwards (InstaColor), Caparol, Tikkurila, Dulux, Sherwin-Williams and Lechler. These should work with OpenColor, though only the Datacolor-branded unit has been verified. Requires Chrome or Edge on desktop or Android — Web Bluetooth is not available on iPhone or iPad.

For developers & AI agents

Match a CIELAB color through the open, key-free API: /api/match?L=53.2&a=80.1&b=67.2 (JSON, CORS-open). Download the full dataset at /data/all.json, or read llms.txt for the overview.

FAQ

Can I use a Datacolor ColorReader without the vendor app?

Yes — OpenColor connects to the ColorReader directly from Chrome or Edge over Web Bluetooth and matches the reading to RAL, Pantone, NCS, Munsell and paint-fandeck standards. No vendor app or account needed.

Does it work with rebranded ColorReaders (PPG, Benjamin Moore, RAL…)?

Those are the same Datacolor CR100 hardware, so they should work. Only the Datacolor unit has been verified so far.

Does OpenColor work on iPhone?

No. Web Bluetooth isn't available in Safari or any iOS browser, so scanning isn't possible on iPhone or iPad. Use Chrome or Edge on Android or a desktop.

Is there an API to match a CIELAB color to standards?

Yes: GET /api/match?L=53.2&a=80.1&b=67.2 returns the nearest standards by CIEDE2000 as JSON. The full dataset is at /data/all.json.

Runs entirely in your browser — scans and history stay on your device. On-screen swatches are sRGB approximations; the CIELAB and CIEDE2000 values are authoritative.

About OpenColor

OpenColor is a free, independent web app that turns a Datacolor ColorReader (and the paint-brand rebrands built on the same CR100 hardware) into a cross-standard color matcher — no vendor app or account required.

It connects to the device over Web Bluetooth in Chrome or Edge, reads the measured CIELAB value, and finds the nearest named colors across ~11,400 references in eight systems (CSS, RAL Classic, Pantone C/U, NCS, Munsell, Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore), ranked by CIEDE2000. It’s installable as a PWA and works offline for browsing.

Privacy: everything runs client-side — your scans and history stay on your device and are never uploaded.

For developers & agents: an open, key-free match API (/api/match) and downloadable datasets (/data/all.json, llms.txt) are available.

Questions or feedback? Email hello@opencolor.app.

Requires Chrome or Edge on desktop or Android. Web Bluetooth is unavailable on iPhone and iPad.
